Neon War TD — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026 · Developer: NeonLab Games · Contact: neonlabgames@gmail.com

Neon War TD ("the game") is a mobile game by NeonLab Games. This policy explains what data the game and its partners process, why, and the choices you have.

1. Data we do not collect

We do not ask for your name, e-mail, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. You can play without an account. Your game progress is stored only on your device.

2. Advertising — Google AdMob

The game shows ads through Google AdMob (banner, interstitial and optional rewarded ads). AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID), IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and ad interaction data, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent with Google's consent form (a certified Consent Management Platform) before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & ads. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

See how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

3. Analytics and configuration — Google Firebase

We use Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is played (for example levels started and finished, ads watched, purchases made) and Firebase Remote Config to adjust game settings without an update. Firebase may process an app instance ID, device and app information and usage events. Analytics data is used only to improve the game. Where consent is required, analytics storage follows your consent choice.

Firebase privacy information: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

4. In-app purchases — Google Play

Purchases (for example "Remove Banner", "No Ads", gems, the Battle Pass and the Starter Pack) are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. We receive only a confirmation of the purchase so the item can be delivered.

5. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules local reminders on your device (for example "Treasury is full"). No data is sent to us for this. You can turn them off in Settings or in Android.

6. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Data retention and deletion

Game progress stays on your device until you uninstall the game or use Settings → Reset progress. Advertising and analytics data are kept by Google according to its policies. To ask about or delete data related to your device, e-mail us.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of your data and to withdraw consent. Contact us at the e-mail above.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
